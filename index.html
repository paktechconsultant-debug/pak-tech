FBR Statutory Penalty Matrix

📋 Income Tax Return late filing

-

Days of Delay: 0

Penalty Liability: PKR 0

Section 182 - FBR Ordinance: Return late-filing carries a penalty calculated at the higher of Rs 1,000 per day of default or 0.1% of tax payable per day, with the total penalty capped at 25% of total tax due.

🛍️ Sales Tax Return late filing

-

Months of Delay: 0

Penalty Liability: PKR 0

FBR Sales Tax Act: Filing after the 18th of the month carries a statutory penalty of 5% of the tax due for the first month, with an additional 5% for each subsequent month of default, subject to a statutory minimum of Rs 10,000.

💰 Withholding Tax (WHT) Default Matrix

-

Penalty Liability: PKR 0

Section 205 - Withholding Default: Defaulting on withholding tax deductions carries a statutory penalty of 10% of the tax amount not deducted, with an additional 10% penalty for each subsequent quarter the default remains uncorrected.
